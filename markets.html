    This isn't a fee: dividends are paid to every share, yours included. Keep 75% of the shares and you get 75% of every dividend; CX takes nothing. The charter is your promise to shareholders, and it can't change once you file. CX adds the same protections to every charter: your pay and other costs are capped, trading halts if a report is 30 days late, no new investor claim can rank above shareholders, the number of shares never changes, and if you close the company its cash is paid out to every share. Fans see your charter before they bid.
